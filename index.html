About Germark Republic

Founded in 2019, Germark Republic is a sovereign digital nation governed by a central digital authority. Our mission is to create a secure, transparent, and inclusive society where every citizen is empowered through technology.

Germark is built on principles of peace, sustainability, and innovation. We believe in digital citizenship, decentralized governance, and collaborative growth. Our infrastructure is designed to support secure identity, digital housing, and civic participation.

Citizens of Germark are issued digital passports, assigned eco-friendly apartments, and can contribute to national news and development. Our founding citizens, Mouhammad and Bjørn, envisioned a republic where technology serves the people — not the other way around.

We welcome institutional partnerships, cultural exchange, and visionary thinkers to help shape Germark’s future. Contact us at Germarkcontact@gmail.com.
